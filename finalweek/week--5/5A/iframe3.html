BigBasket

BigBasket is an Indian online grocery delivery service that was founded in 2011. It offers a wide range of products, including fresh fruits and vegetables, dairy products, snacks, beverages, and household essentials. BigBasket operates in several cities across India and has become one of the leading players in the online grocery market. The company provides convenient and efficient grocery shopping experiences to customers through its website and mobile app, allowing them to order groceries from the comfort of their homes and have them delivered to their doorstep.
